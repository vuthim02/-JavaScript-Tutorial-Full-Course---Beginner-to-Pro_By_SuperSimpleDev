Key Lessons From Each

🔷 From Stripe

Professional SaaS needs trust. Use clean typography, generous spacing, and one accent color. Every section should have ONE clear action. Hierarchy guides the eye naturally.

🟠 From Craigslist

Ugly can be functional. Power users want density and speed. But for 99% of products, this design kills conversion. Study it to understand what NOT to do for modern products.

🔵 From Apple

Less is more. One product per screen. Huge text. Massive images. Minimal color. Let the product speak. The absence of clutter IS the design. Premium = restraint.
